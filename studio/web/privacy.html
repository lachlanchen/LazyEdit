Private Studio, your media

LazyEdit Studio is an invite-only video editing and publishing service operated by LazyingArt LLC. Each invited account has a separate workspace. Other users cannot access the operator's private Pi.

What is stored

Your chosen videos, transcripts, corrections, metadata, outputs, publication jobs and platform browser profiles are stored in your private workspace. The HTTPS gateway forwards uploads without retaining media. Studio passwords and access tokens are hashed. Optional Apple/Google linking stores provider identity IDs; necessary provider refresh credentials are encrypted. Technical logs contain connection timing and errors.

Processing and publication

Configured transcription, translation and AI providers receive the inputs needed for the processing steps you request. Publishing sends your selected media and metadata to your selected platforms using your connected accounts. Uploading does not itself publish. Platform login is an authenticated browser view restricted to supported sites.

Your controls and deletion

Sign out or revoke linked applications to stop new requests. Hiding a video retains its files and history. Invited users can permanently delete their account in the native Account screen or web account page, confirming their username and Studio password. Active processing/publication must finish first. Deletion immediately revokes Studio access, closes desktop viewing, and schedules private workspace, platform profile and media removal. Minimal anonymous transaction/account tombstones may remain to prevent replay. Deletion does not remove posts already published to external platforms; manage those with the relevant platform. Operator service retirement is handled separately to protect the original backend and Pi.

Billing and notifications

The app download uses store pricing. Monthly purchases and paid-download credit are not active in the current pilot. When enabled, purchases use the platform store and server verification, with purchase IDs and entitlement records retained for billing integrity. Login notifications are opt-in, generic, and currently work while Studio is in the foreground. No credentials or QR codes appear in notifications. There are no ads or tracking SDKs.

Contact contact@lazying.art for data questions, deletion problems or service retirement. Updated 2026-10-04.
