Private Studio, your media

LazyEdit Studio is a private account-based video editing and publishing service operated by LazyingArt LLC. This beta is for invited owner testing; public account registration is unavailable.

What is stored

Videos you choose, transcripts, corrections, metadata, render outputs, publication jobs and account authorization records are stored on the Studio workstation. The HTTPS gateway forwards traffic; it does not retain uploaded video files. Passwords are stored as salted hashes; access credentials are stored as hashes. Connection logs may include request timing and technical errors.

Processing and publication

Your configured transcription, translation and AI providers may receive the inputs necessary for the processing steps you request. The providers depend on your Studio configuration. Publishing sends your selected media and metadata to the chosen platforms using your connected accounts. Uploading a file does not itself publish it.

Your controls

You can revoke linked applications from Account → Connect and sign out. Revocation prevents new requests but does not cancel already submitted jobs or delete media. Removing a video from the library currently hides its database entry; it does not erase all files or published posts. Contact the operator to request permanent deletion of media or your account.

There are no ads, purchase flow, tracking SDKs or public signup in this beta. Internet access is required; private media is not cached for offline use.
